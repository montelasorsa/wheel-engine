# Decision Log SOP

**Scaffold — the procedure body is not written yet.** It defines the shape of a
log entry: the situation, the options considered, the rule IDs cited, the
decision taken, and the operator's reasoning in their own words.

The reasoning field is the point. A logged decision without it cannot be
reviewed later, which defeats `/wheel-engine:wheel-review`.
